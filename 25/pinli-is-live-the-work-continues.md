# Pinli is live: the work continues

Earlier this month, I wrote about [ten years' worth of ideas that I didn’t ship](https://bosnadev.com/2026/09/01/ten-years-of-ideas-i-didnt-ship). Today, I’m excited to share one that people can actually use: [Pinli](https://pinli.co).

Pinli is a platform where you can create a personalized page showcasing your work. You can combine links, images, products, writing, bookings, and ways for others to get in touch, all while arranging the page for both mobile and larger screens. Published pages are also accessible through the Explore feature.

I wanted to create something that offers more flexibility than a simple list of links, but without requiring every individual to build and maintain an entire website.

Now that it's live, I can finally receive feedback on the parts that may need improvement—areas I may have overlooked. I'm eager to see the pages you create, identify where the editor may hinder you, and find out which features are missing. Your observations will guide my future development work.

Pinli has grown beyond the small experiments I discussed in that initial essay. I’m thrilled it has reached a stage where others can use it. Bosnadev will continue to be my outlet for discussing the decisions behind the work, sharing failures, and documenting the other products and client projects I am working on alongside it.

If you create a Pinli page, please share it with me. I’d love to see what you’ve included and hear what challenges you faced in the process.
